1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information

  • Your name, email address and, if you register with email, your password (stored only in hashed form).
  • If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Your profile details, such as display name and avatar.

1.2 Gameplay, coins and battles

  • Quiz answers, scores, daily challenge results, streaks, achievements, rankings and leaderboard position.
  • Coin battles: battles you create or join, your opponents, answers, scores, results and the coins involved in each battle.
  • Spin wheel results.
  • Coin balance and history, redeem requests, and the gift card or voucher codes issued to you.
  • Your referral code and the referral code you signed up with.

1.3 Posts, comments and likes

  • Comments you write on posts and blogs in the app, the time you posted them, and the posts you like or save.

1.4 Device and technical information

  • A device identifier, device model, operating system version and IP address when you sign in. We use these to keep your account secure and to limit each device to 3 accounts.
  • A push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), so we can send notifications you have allowed.
  • App version, crash and error information.

1.5 Analytics data (Firebase Analytics)

  • We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used. It collects an app instance ID, device and app information, approximate location (country/region, derived from IP address), session information, and events such as screens viewed, quizzes started or completed, battles played and features used.
  • Firebase Analytics does not receive your password, and we do not send your name or email address to it.

1.6 Advertising data

  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob and the Meta (Facebook) SDK may collect your device’s Android advertising ID, IP address, and ad and app interaction data to show, measure and personalize ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, SMS or call logs.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To run quizzes, daily challenges, coin battles and the spin wheel, track scores and streaks, and show leaderboards.
  • To manage your coin balance and process redeem requests.
  • To publish and display your comments and likes on posts.
  • To send push notifications about challenges, battle invites, rewards and app updates.
  • To understand app usage with Firebase Analytics and improve features, content and performance.
  • To show ads that keep MovieMind free.
  • To prevent fraud, cheating, multiple-account abuse and misuse of rewards and referrals.
  • To moderate comments and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

3. Information other users can see

  • Leaderboards: your display name, avatar, score and rank.
  • Coin battles: your opponents see your display name, avatar, score and battle result.
  • Comments: comments you post are public to other MovieMind users, together with your display name and avatar. Please do not include personal information in comments.

We may remove comments that are abusive, hateful, sexually explicit, spam or otherwise break our Terms, and we may suspend accounts that repeatedly do so. To report a comment, contact info@bytebodh.in.

5. Android permissions

  • Internet and network state to load quizzes, battles, posts and ads.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later) to send you push notifications. You can turn this off at any time.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) for ads and ad measurement.

6. Data security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections, passwords are hashed, sign-in tokens are stored in secure device storage, and access to our database is restricted. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Export your data: Account → Data & Account → Export my data.
  • Delete your account: Account → Data & Account → Delete account permanently deletes your account inside the app. You can also request deletion by emailing info@bytebodh.in from your registered email address.
  • Correct your details: Account → Edit Profile.
  • Delete a comment: remove your own comments from the post where you wrote them, or contact us.
  • Notifications: turn off in Android Settings → Apps → MovieMind → Notifications.
  • Ads personalization: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

You can email info@bytebodh.in for any privacy request. We reply within 30 days.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, email, sign-in details, wallet, coin and reward history, quiz, challenge and spin history, notifications, comments and likes.

  • Device identifiers (without IP addresses) are kept to prevent misuse of the 3-accounts-per-device limit.
  • Where other players took part in the same coin battle or multiplayer game, that game record stays and shows you as “Deleted User”, with no personal details.
  • Firebase Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in our Firebase project (up to 14 months) and is not linked to your name or email.
  • We may keep limited records longer where required by law or to resolve disputes and prevent fraud.

9. Children

MovieMind is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact info@bytebodh.in and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Our servers are located in India. Our service providers, including Google/Firebase and Meta, may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date and tell you in the app about important changes.
